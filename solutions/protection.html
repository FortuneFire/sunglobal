PROTECTION

Protect what matters most.

Protection cover helps provide financial security when life changes. The right solution depends on your circumstances, responsibilities and priorities.

Review Your Protection → Explore All Solutions →

Life Cover

Life cover can provide financial support for dependants or loved ones if the insured person passes away. It can help cover ongoing expenses and provide peace of mind.

Funeral Cover

Funeral cover can help meet the costs associated with a funeral and lessen the pressure on family members at a difficult time.

Disability & Income Protection

Coverage for disability or loss of income can help support an individual and their household if they are unable to work for a period of time.

Vehicle & Personal Insurance

Insurance options for vehicles and personal assets can help protect day-to-day living and important responsibilities.

REVIEWING EXISTING COVER

Your circumstances change. Your cover should be reviewed too.

Protection needs are not static. Changes in income, family circumstances, debt, responsibilities or assets may affect the level of cover that is appropriate. Regular reviews can help keep cover aligned with the current stage of life.

It is often helpful to revisit protection needs after a major life event, such as marriage, the arrival of children, a new purchase, a salary increase or a change in household structure.
