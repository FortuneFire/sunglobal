REVIEWING EXISTING ARRANGEMENTS

Retirement planning is more than choosing a number.

It involves looking at current savings, expected retirement age, desired income, existing arrangements, short-term needs and the broader life you want to support. The right strategy depends on your personal circumstances and the financial decisions you are making today.

Good retirement planning often includes a review of what is already in place, how money is invested, what income could be needed later and what needs to be adjusted to better support the future.
