STRUCTURED INVESTMENTS

Structured investments for more specialised goals.

Structured investments can suit clients with specific investment objectives and a need to consider defined terms, characteristics and risk considerations in a more detailed way.

Index-linked Structures

Structured products may be linked to indices or other market measures, with specific terms that can affect the value outcome.

Capital-protected Structures

Some structures may offer capital protection features, though the terms and conditions will vary depending on the specific product.

Geared-growth Structures

Geared or leveraged structures can amplify exposure and require careful assessment of suitability and risk.

Digital / Barrier Structures

Digital or barrier-style investments may involve particular conditions affecting outcomes at maturity or on specific events.

UNDERSTAND THE STRUCTURE

Before committing to a structure, understand the risk.

Structured investments may involve market risk, issuer or counterparty risk, liquidity considerations, defined investment periods, product-specific conditions and the possibility of loss of capital depending on the structure. The exact risks will depend on the specific product and its terms.

Thorough understanding matters because the features that make a structure attractive for one client may not be suitable for another.
